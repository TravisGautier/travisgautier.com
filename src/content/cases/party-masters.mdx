## Overview

Party Masters is a party-rental and DJ company that ran bookings, quotes,
invoices and inventory across spreadsheets, email threads and a stack of
disconnected tools. The engagement replaces all of it with one custom CRM,
built by an agent team under the same pipeline the channel teaches: discovery,
failing tests first, implementation, refactor, commit, with the client's
feedback triaged into sprints.

The first release went to the client and came back with 25 items of feedback.
21 shipped in the next sprint. The 4 that did not are all payments work, and
payments is the phase in progress now.
